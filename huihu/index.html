Context & Problem

背景与问题

L4 已经上路，私家车座舱还没准备好。L4 is already on the road. The private-car cabin is not yet ready for it.文案据 08_copy/ch02_context.md

01

L4 来了，但先来的是出租车

L4 Has Arrived, as a Taxi First

L4 已在限定区域规模化运营：Waymo 每周提供 40 万次以上付费出行S19，萝卜快跑覆盖 28 城、全无人驾驶里程超 2.4 亿公里S16，文远知行的 L4 车队约 3,400 台S18。私家车这边，中国首批 L3 车型 2025 年 12 月才获附条件准入，且限定在指定路段S3。面向私家车的 L4 座舱，仍是一个前瞻命题。

L4 already runs at scale within defined areas: Waymo delivers over 400,000 paid rides a weekS19, Apollo Go operates in 28 cities with more than 240 million fully driverless kilometresS16, and WeRide runs an L4 fleet of about 3,400 vehiclesS18. For private cars, China granted its first conditional L3 approvals only in December 2025, limited to designated roadsS3. An L4 cabin for the private car is still a question about the future.

02

方向盘第一次可以离开

The Wheel Can Finally Step Away

GB 17675-2025 删除了转向系必须保留机械连接的强制要求，并对线控转向提出失效降级与报警要求，2026 年 7 月 1 日实施S6。没有了机械管柱，方向盘才有收纳的可能。

GB 17675-2025, in force from 1 July 2026, removes the requirement for a mechanical steering link and sets fallback and warning rules for steer-by-wireS6. Without a mechanical column, the wheel can finally be stowed.

03

三个问题

Three Problems

  1. P1

    谁在驾驶，并不总是清楚。研究发现，驾驶人对“干预后谁在控车”的理解与量产车的实际逻辑存在错位S35；逐级切换式 HMI 的模式混淆率是按功能分区 HMI 的 2 倍，加入视觉反馈后明显下降S36。GB 44721-2026 也要求明确提示系统的就绪、激活、退出状态，并告知用户角色转换S7。

    Who is driving is not always clear. Drivers' understanding of who controls the car after an intervention does not match how production cars actually behaveS35; a level-by-level HMI produces twice the mode confusion of a function-based one, and visual feedback reduces it markedlyS36. GB 44721-2026 requires clear ready, active and exit states, and that users be told when their role changesS7.

  2. P2

    屏幕吞掉了按键。触屏中控带来更多、更长的视线离路S37；Euro NCAP 2026 起要求转向灯、双闪等基础功能使用直接物理输入S20S21。

    Screens have swallowed the buttons. Touch-based centre stacks draw longer and more frequent glances away from the roadS37, and from 2026 Euro NCAP requires direct physical inputs for basic functions such as indicators and hazard lightsS20S21.

  3. P3

    空出来的时间，座舱接不住。一项覆盖 47 项研究的荟萃分析显示，非驾驶活动出现率从 L3 的 27% 升至 L4 的 41%S30；另一项调查发现，人们设想在自动驾驶中工作、阅读、娱乐，却认为车内时间更不“有用”，作者归因于缺少合适的内饰S33。

    The cabin cannot hold the time it frees up. A meta-analysis of 47 studies shows non-driving activities rising from 27% at L3 to 41% at L4S30; another survey found people expect to work, read and be entertained in automated cars, yet rate the time as less useful, which the authors attribute to unsuitable interiorsS33.

L4 中，人们想做什么

What People Want to Do in L4

荟萃分析显示，L4 中最常见的是看风景（约 80%）与被动娱乐（约 78%），也有人睡觉（约 31%）或用电脑工作（约 26%）S30。在仍保留方向盘的 L4 中，超过八成的人选择看向窗外，部分原因是想保持对路况的感知S30。

In L4, the most common activities are looking at the scenery (about 80%) and passive entertainment (about 78%); some sleep (about 31%) or work on a laptop (about 26%)S30. Where L4 cars keep a steering wheel, more than 80% choose to look out of the window, partly to stay aware of the roadS30.

信任仍是门槛：2025 年仅 13% 的美国驾驶人表示信任自动驾驶车S27。

Trust is still the barrier: in 2025 only 13% of US drivers said they trust self-driving vehiclesS27.

其余场景（高速长途、驻车等候、紧急降级）待补充；驻车等候为设计假设。Further scenarios (long-distance motorway, parked waiting, emergency fallback) to follow; parked waiting is a design assumption.

04

Design Strategy

设计策略

三条策略，回应同一个问题：当驾驶时有时无，座舱如何保持秩序。Three strategies answer one question: how a cabin keeps its order when driving comes and goes.文案据 08_copy/ch04_strategy.md

  1. S1

    一盘两态

    One Wheel, Two Modes

    方向盘展开即人工驾驶，收起即进入 L4。模式切换由一个看得见的实体动作完成，驾驶员不必读屏也知道当前由谁驾驶。

    Deployed, the wheel means manual driving; stowed, it means L4. The change of mode is a visible physical act, so the driver knows who is in control without reading a screen.

  2. S2

    屏有分区，键有其位

    Zoned Display, Placed Controls

    大连屏为规则长八边形，分为驾驶主控、中央共享、副驾娱乐三区，状态只在这里显示。操作分两处：基础驾驶功能（双闪、SOS、转向灯、挡位、驾驶辅助激活等）集中在驾驶员左侧的实体键区，位置固定、带触觉反馈；方向盘展开 / 收起两种状态下的可达性仍有布局冲突，待验证；其余触控按钮在左右门板，门板不显示任何状态。每一次操作，都在大屏对应分区得到回应。操作在手边，信息在眼前。

    A regular elongated-octagon display is divided into three zones: driver control, shared centre, passenger entertainment. Status appears only here. Controls sit in two places: essential driving functions (hazards, SOS, indicators, gear, driver-assist activation) are physical keys in a panel on the driver's left, fixed in place, with haptic feedback; reach in both wheel states still has a layout conflict and is pending validation; all other touch controls are on the door panels, which never show status. Every input is answered in the matching zone of the display. Controls at hand, information in view.

    实体键区参照工信部 GB 4094 修订征求意见稿（2026 年 2 月，尚未正式发布）对实体操纵件的要求S8。

    The physical key panel follows the requirements for physical controls in MIIT's draft revision of GB 4094 (February 2026, not yet final)S8.

  3. S3

    以驾驶员为中心的秩序

    Driver-Centred Order

    中控朝向驾驶员，中央区域不设按钮，实体键也让到驾驶员左侧。造型以规则弧线与统一切角组织层次，拒绝不规则曲面：规则，而不呆板。

    The centre console turns toward the driver; no buttons sit in the middle, and even the physical keys move to the driver's left. Form is organised by regular arcs and consistent chamfers, never free-form surfaces: regular, but not rigid.

人工与 L4 之间的切换也在大屏左区逐步提示：进入 L4 时，先移座椅（后移并下降），再收方向盘；切回人工时，先展开方向盘，再复位座椅。座椅后移 / 下降行程待核定

Switching between manual and L4 is shown step by step in the left zone: entering L4, the seat moves first (back and down), then the wheel stows; returning to manual, the wheel deploys first, then the seat resets. Seat travel pending validation

状态统一交给中央长八边形大屏。大屏分三区：左区驾驶主控，中区共享信息，右区副驾娱乐。无论按下实体键还是触控门板，都会在对应分区得到明确反馈；操作与结果不在同一处，却始终一一对应。

All status lives on the central elongated-octagon display, divided into driver control on the left, shared information in the centre and passenger entertainment on the right. Whether a physical key or a door control is pressed, the matching zone responds clearly. Action and result sit apart, yet always correspond one to one.

Compliance · GB 4094 征求意见稿

实体键区参照工信部 GB 4094 修订征求意见稿（2026 年 2 月，尚未正式发布）中“实体操纵件不应仅设置在显示屏上”的要求S8。

The physical key panel follows the draft revision of GB 4094, published for comment by MIIT in February 2026 and not yet final, which requires that certain controls not exist only on a screenS8.
